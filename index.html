Fotofold

Years of screenshots, sorted in an afternoon.

What it does

Fotofold sorts the photos in your library into real albums in the Photos app. It already guesses the category; you swipe to confirm.

  • Swipe right to file it into the suggested album (tap another category to change it), left to delete.
  • One-tap sort files the photos it’s sure about and never deletes anything.
  • Every category is a real album. Delete the app and your albums stay.
  • No sign-up, no ads.

FAQ

How do points work?

Swiping one by one is always free. One-tap sort charges before it starts, by how many photos it will sort: 1 point per 20 photos with standard categories only, 1 point per 8 photos when advanced ones (like Outfits, Makeup, Concerts) are included. You see the photo count and points before you start, and sorting the same photo again isn't charged twice. Filing photos into albums and deleting them are free. New users get 100 points, enough for about 2,000 photos. Point packs (300, 1,000 and 2,000) are one-time purchases, not subscriptions, and points never expire.

What is the photo check? Can I turn it off?

With your permission, reduced-size copies of some photos are sent to our server and analyzed by Anthropic’s AI service, Claude, to sort them. We don’t keep them; Anthropic doesn’t train on them and deletes them automatically, usually within 30 days. Turn it off anytime in Settings → Points; sorting with the default categories keeps working, but adding new categories needs your permission. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Where are my albums?

In the Photos app: Albums tab, near the bottom of the list.

Will Fotofold delete my photos?

Only photos you swipe left, and only after you confirm in the iOS dialog (once per round). They go to Recently Deleted and can be recovered for 30 days. One-tap sort never deletes.

I got a new iPhone. Are my points still there?

Points belong to an anonymous ID kept in iCloud Keychain. If iCloud Keychain is on with the same Apple Account, your points come with you. If they don’t, email us.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone; the albums stay in Photos. To delete your anonymous ID, points and records on our server, email us from Settings → Contact Us (the email includes your anonymous ID). We delete them within 30 days (remaining points are removed too).
