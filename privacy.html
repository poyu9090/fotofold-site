Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Fotofold (“we”) helps you sort the photos on your iPhone or iPad into albums. We collect as little as possible: no sign-up, no ads, no tracking. This policy explains what data the app uses, what is sent to our server and our service providers, and how to delete it.

At a glance

  • No account. We don’t ask for your name, email or phone number; Fotofold uses a random anonymous ID.
  • With your permission, reduced-size copies of some of your photos are sent to our server and analyzed by a third-party AI service to sort them. We don’t keep them.
  • No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

1. Your photo library

With your permission, Fotofold reads your photo library to show your photos, sort them, create albums in the Photos app and add photos to them. Which album each photo went into is stored on your device. A photo is deleted only when you swipe it left and confirm in the iOS system dialog.

2. Photo analysis by a third-party AI service (with your consent)

To sort your photos into categories, Fotofold may send reduced-size copies of some of your photos to our server, which passes them to Anthropic’s AI service, Claude, to tell which category each photo belongs to. Nothing is sent until you agree on the consent screen when you first open the app.

What the consent screen in the app says (word for word):

A few photos it isn't sure about are shrunk and sent through our server to Anthropic's AI, Claude, which decides the category.

We don't keep your photos. Anthropic usually deletes them within 30 days and doesn't train on them. You can turn this off anytime in Settings.

What's sent
A few photos it isn't sure about while sorting. They're shrunk first and sent without location or date.
Who gets them?
First to our server (which doesn't keep photos), then Anthropic's AI, Claude, tells which category they belong to.
How long is it kept?
Anthropic usually deletes them within 30 days, except content that violates its usage policy. Your photos aren't used to train AI.
Can I turn it off?
Settings → Points → "Send unsure photos for checking", anytime. Once it's off, nothing more is sent, and you can't add categories.
  • What is sent: a reduced-size copy of each photo, without location, date or other photo information, together with the category to check. Your anonymous ID is not passed on to Anthropic.
  • Not kept: our server handles the photos only to return the result and does not save them or write them to logs. Anthropic processes them for us, does not use them to train its models, and deletes them automatically, usually within 30 days. Content flagged as violating Anthropic’s usage policy may be kept for up to 2 years (Anthropic).
  • Your choice: you can withdraw consent anytime in Settings → Points. After that, no more photos are sent. Sorting with the default categories keeps working, but adding new categories requires consent.

3. Anonymous ID

On first launch Fotofold creates a random ID and keeps it in your iCloud Keychain. It is sent with requests to our server and attached to your purchases so we can keep your points balance. It is not your Apple Account, name, email or phone number, and it is never used to identify you across other apps or websites.

4. Points and purchases

Points are bought through Apple. Apple handles payment, and we never see your card or Apple Account details. After a purchase, the app sends Apple’s signed transaction record to our server, which stores the transaction ID, the pack and the points added to your anonymous ID.

5. Preventing abuse

New users get 100 free points once per device. To stop repeat claims and to check that requests come from a genuine copy of Fotofold, we use Apple’s DeviceCheck and App Attest services. They let Apple confirm that the device and the app are genuine without telling us who you are, and we don’t store a device identifier.

6. Records on our server

  • The points ledger for your anonymous ID: purchases, welcome points and points used.
  • For each photo filed into an album: the photo’s internal identifier from iOS, the category code and the points used, so the same photo is charged only once per category. This is not the photo or its contents.
  • How many photos were analyzed per day and per minute, to prevent abuse.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts our server, processes your IP address to deliver requests. We don’t store it or use it to find your location.

7. Anonymous usage statistics

We use TelemetryDeck to understand how the app is used, for example how many sorting rounds are finished. Signals contain only event names, rough ranges, device model and system version, and an anonymized device identifier that TelemetryDeck uses to count users. They contain no photos, no album names and nothing that identifies you, and they are not linked to your anonymous ID.

8. No tracking, no ads

Fotofold does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), does not track you across apps or websites, shows no ads, and never sells or shares data for advertising.

9. Who processes data

  • Cloudflare hosts our server and database.
  • Anthropic analyzes photos, only if you agree (section 2).
  • Apple processes payments and provides DeviceCheck and App Attest (section 5).
  • TelemetryDeck receives anonymous usage statistics (section 7).

These providers may process data in the United States and other countries, and may use it only to provide their service to us.

10. Keeping and deleting data

  • On your device: deleting Fotofold removes its data. The albums it created stay in the Photos app; delete them there if you like. The anonymous ID may stay in your iCloud Keychain so your points come back if you reinstall.
  • Photos sent for analysis: we don’t keep them; Anthropic deletes them automatically, usually within 30 days (section 2).
  • Points, purchases and records on our server: kept while you use your anonymous ID. To have them deleted, email us from Settings → Contact Us (the email includes your anonymous ID so we can find your records). We delete them within 30 days, and any remaining points are removed too.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw consent at any time (this doesn’t affect what was done before). Email us to use these rights. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. We analyze photos based on your consent, handle points and purchases to provide the service you asked for, and keep abuse-prevention records and anonymous statistics based on our legitimate interest in running a reliable app.

12. Children

Fotofold is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

13. Changes

If we change what is sent or whom it is sent to, the app asks for your consent again before sending anything. Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new effective date.
